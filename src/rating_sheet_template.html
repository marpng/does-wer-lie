How to rate

Listen to the audio, then judge whether the model output keeps the meaning of what was said. Judge meaning, not spelling: differences in punctuation, capitals or number format are not meaning errors by themselves. Tick the flag if the audio or the reference itself is faulty. Ratings are saved in this browser as you go.
